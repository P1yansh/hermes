# Voice DNA: [Author Name / Register Name]

## 1. Core Persona & Communication Stance
- **Who is speaking**: [e.g. Pragmatic college software engineer, deadpan senior peer]
- **Primary objective**: [e.g. Deliver maximum technical clarity with zero corporate marketing fluff]
- **Emotional posture**: [e.g. Direct, honest about trade-offs and bugs, skeptical of unmeasured claims]

## 2. Quantitative Targets & Cadence
- **Target Mean Sentence Length**: 16 - 20 words
- **Burstiness (Standard Deviation)**: > 10 words (ensures varied rhythm)
- **Staccato Sentences (<=6 words)**: ~10% (use for punchy declarations)
- **Extended Sentences (>=28 words)**: ~15% (use for step-by-step causal explanations)
- **Contraction Rate**: > 85% ("don't", "it's", "we've", "didn't")
- **Epistemic Stance**: Calibrated uncertainty (admit what didn't work before celebrating what did)

## 3. Diction, Tone & Register
- **Register**: Conversational technical
- **Diction Texture**: Concrete tools, terminal commands, and numbers over abstract adjectives
- **Humor & Subtext**: Deadpan, understated, self-deprecating

## 4. Punctuation & Formatting Conventions
- **Punctuation Rules**: Max 1-2 em dashes per article; prefer periods and parentheses for asides
- **Visual Scannability**: Short 2-3 sentence paragraphs, comparison tables, code blocks

## 5. Negative Space (Hard Prohibitions)
- **Banned Openers**: "In today's fast-paced world", "It is worth noting that", "Clearly", "Honestly"
- **Banned Words**: delve, leverage, seamless, robust, testament, tapestry, supercharge, elevate, vital
- **Banned Structures**: "Not only X, but also Y", rhetorical hype questions, announcement colons
